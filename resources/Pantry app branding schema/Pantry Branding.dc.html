Brand style guide

Pantry

A recipe assistant that reads like a note from someone who actually cooks. Pantry takes a link or a scrap of pasted text and turns it into a calm, scannable recipe — plus the "why" behind it — without ever feeling clinical.

Cozy Homey Plain-spoken

Naming & mark exploration

Four ways to say it

Each mark is built from two soft, overlapping shapes — never a literal icon — so it sits comfortably inside Organic's over-rounded language. "Pantry" is the working name and reads as the strongest fit: plain, warm, and already the room the app lives in.

Pantry

Recommended

The shelf itself. Two shapes — jar body, herb sprig — read as "well-stocked, home-cooked" at any size.

Hearth & Ladle

A ladle silhouette in two strokes. Warmer and more literal — leans into the "grandma's kitchen" end of the brand.

Sift

Concentric rings, like mesh caught mid-shake. Quieter and more minimal — a calmer, more editorial read.

Kettle & Kin

A kettle spout tipped mid-pour. "Kin" nods at the cookbook/saved-recipes feature — food shared with people.

Color

Ground, and two accents doing real work

Pantry adds one guest color to Organic's pair — a browned-butter amber — reserved entirely for the Reaction glossary category, so it reads as a deliberate third voice rather than decoration.
